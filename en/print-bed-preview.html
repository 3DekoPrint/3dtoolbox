How it works

  1. Choose your printer: the bed is shown at your printer’s size.
  2. Load a file: the model appears in the middle of the bed. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
  3. Orient: lay it flat on its largest face or rotate in 90° steps. Red means it does not fit.
  4. Choose a material: you see weight, print time and material cost as a guide.

Good to know

  • The preview does not replace a slicer. Supports, brim, layer lines and exact times only come there.
  • For multi-colour prints with nozzle changes (e.g. H2C) the usable build volume can be smaller than shown here.
  • More help on sizes and clearances: tolerances and wall thickness and the lexicon.
