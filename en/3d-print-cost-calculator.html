How it's calculated: Material = weight (g) × price/kg ÷ 1,000. Electricity = power (W) × time (h) ÷ 1,000 × electricity price. Wear = purchase price ÷ lifetime (h) × print time (h) — reflects the printer's wear over its lifetime. The failure rate is added as a buffer on top of the material, electricity and wear subtotal. In seller mode, labour time, packaging and margin are added on top; the marketplace fee is calculated so your target margin still holds after the fee is deducted. All calculations run locally in your browser — no data is transmitted.
