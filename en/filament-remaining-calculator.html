How the calculator works

Weigh the spool with the remaining filament on a kitchen scale and subtract the empty spool weight. The result is the remaining amount in grams.

Length follows from weight, material density and diameter: length = mass ÷ (density × cross-section area). Everything is calculated locally in your browser.

Tip: enter the weight from your slicer – the calculator shows whether the spool is enough or whether you should open a new one to avoid a mid-print swap.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the empty spool weight?

It is sometimes printed on the spool label. Otherwise weigh an empty spool of the same brand – typically 120 to 250 grams.

How many metres are in 1 kg of filament?

For 1.75 mm PLA (density about 1.24 g/cm³) roughly 335 metres, PETG about 330 metres, ABS about 400 metres.

Is the value exact?

No, it is an approximation: density and diameter vary slightly by manufacturer and colour.
