How it works

Pick shape and size, a raised rim against condensation and a pattern on the surface. Engrave a name or word in the centre.

Up to 8 coasters make a set in one file.

Printing notes

  • Print flat with the surface up. The pattern is engraved and needs no supports.
  • Use PETG for hot drinks; PLA softens from about 55 °C.
  • A strip of felt or silicone under the coaster protects the table.

Common questions

Which size fits?

For mugs and glasses 90 to 110 mm, for cans 85 mm.

Can I engrave text?

Yes, just type text. The text is 0.8 mm deep. Short words look best.
