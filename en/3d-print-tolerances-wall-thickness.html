Tolerances and minimum wall thickness

Guide values for FDM prints so parts fit the first time and do not break.

Dimensional accuracy: what to expect

A well-tuned FDM printer usually hits dimensions within about ±0.1 to ±0.3 mm. Outer dimensions are often slightly large, holes and inner contours slightly small. Heat, material shrinkage and the first layer (elephant foot) play a part. Always allow clearance for fits and print a small test piece.

Guide values for clearance and holes

UseGuide value
Moving parts, lids, snap fits0.2 to 0.3 mm clearance
Clean, lightly sliding fit0.1 to 0.2 mm clearance
Press fit0 to 0.1 mm, only after testing
Hole for a screwscrew diameter + 0.3 to 0.5 mm (M3: about 3.4 mm)
Hole for a magnet or heat-set nutsize + 0.1 to 0.2 mm, then test
Small holes under 3 mmtend to close up, better to drill out

Minimum walls and fine details

FeatureGuide value (0.4 mm nozzle)
Wall, simpleat least 0.8 mm (two lines); multiples of the line width print cleanest
Wall, strong1.2 to 2 mm
Free-standing thin pins or ribsat least about 1.5 mm, otherwise they snap easily
Text and embossingstroke width from about 0.8 mm, height from 0.6 mm
Overhang without supportup to about 45 °
Bridge without supportshort spans up to about 5 to 10 mm print cleanly

You can set wall thickness and clearance directly in our generators. Terms such as elephant foot or retraction are explained in the lexicon.

How to test a fit

  1. Print a small test piece with just the critical dimensions (only the pin and the hole).
  2. Measure with calipers and correct the clearance by the measured deviation.
  3. Only then print the large part. This saves material and time.

Frequently asked questions

What tolerance does a 3D printer have?

Typically about ±0.1 to ±0.3 mm, depending on printer, material and calibration.

How much clearance do two mating parts need?

For moving parts and lids usually 0.2 to 0.3 mm. For tighter fits, check with a test piece first.

What minimum wall thickness makes sense?

At least 0.8 mm, for strong parts 1.2 to 2 mm. A multiple of the nozzle width prints cleanest.

Why are my holes too small?

FDM printing often makes inner contours slightly small. Add 0.3 to 0.5 mm for screw holes.

All values are practical guide values for FDM printers with a 0.4 mm nozzle. Every printer, filament and slicer behaves a little differently: always print a small test piece for fits.
