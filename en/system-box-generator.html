System box

A stackable box with lid for tools, accessories and small parts. Set length, width and height, add hand slots and dividers.

STL + 3MFExport

in browserRuns

How it works

The boxes stack on their own: the underside has a recess that takes the raised plate on the lid of the box below. Equal length and width are enough, height may differ.

This is our own system and not compatible with Systainer, Bosch SystemBox or other manufacturer systems. All boxes from this generator fit each other.

Printing notes

  • Print box and lid with the opening facing up, no supports.
  • For heavy tools use 4 walls and 20 % infill, material PETG.
  • The divider rails take plates made with the sorting tray generator.
  • Large boxes need a large bed. The preview shows whether the box fits.

Common questions

Does it fit Festool or Bosch?

No. It is our own open system. The boxes only stack with each other.

Does the lid hold?

It sits with a light press fit. For transport I recommend a rubber band or Velcro strap around the box.
