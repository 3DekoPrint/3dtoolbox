Window plate with curved slot

A flat plate with a curved slot. Slide it over a pin or screw head on the frame and turn it until the window no longer opens.

STL + 3MFExport
0 €Cost
in browserRuns

How it works

The slot starts at the plate edge, runs straight in and then bends. When turned, the pin on the window moves into the curve and the plate locks.

Enter the diameter of your pin or screw head, the generator adds the clearance.

Printing notes

  • Secures against accidental opening and rattling, not burglar protection.
  • Escape windows must not be blocked.
  • PETG or ASA, at least 4 walls so the slot edge holds.
  • Presets are starting values, measure pin and spacing on your window.

Common questions

Which pin fits?

Anything from 4 to 14 mm diameter. Measure with calipers.

How tight is the plate?

The tighter the curve and the smaller the clearance, the tighter. 0.3 mm clearance is snug.
