How it works

The clip holds cables on a desk, wall or the back of furniture. The cable diameter sets the ring size and the opening on top lets you snap the cable in.

Choose countersunk screw tabs or a flat underside for double-sided tape.

Printing notes

  • Print flat with the rings pointing up, no supports needed.
  • PETG or TPU flex better than PLA and are less likely to snap.
  • If the clip is too tight, add 0.2 mm to the cable diameter.

Common questions

How thick is a USB cable?

Most USB cables are 3 to 4.5 mm, power cables 6 to 8 mm, HDMI about 6 mm.

Does it hold well?

Very well with screws. For tape, use good mounting tape and clean the surface first.
