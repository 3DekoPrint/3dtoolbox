What is PC (polycarbonate) used for?

Heat and impact loaded parts: fan shrouds, levers, housings, hinges

+ Pros

Very tough, high strength, HDT ca. 111–117 °C, good electrical insulation

– Cons

Strong warping → enclosure required, very hygroscopic, high temperatures, expensive

Printing tips at a glance

  • Nozzle temperature: 260–290 °C – start in the middle of the range on a new spool and fine-tune with a temperature tower.
  • Bed temperature: 100–120 °C.
  • An enclosed chamber is strongly recommended, otherwise expect warping and layer cracks.
  • Drying: 80 °C, 8 h. Wet filament shows up as crackling, stringing and rough surfaces.

Frequently asked questions

What nozzle temperature does PC (polycarbonate) need?

Guideline for PC (polycarbonate): 260–290 °C nozzle and 100–120 °C bed temperature. The exact values are printed on the manufacturer's spool.

How heat resistant is PC (polycarbonate)?

Heat deflection temperature (HDT) is around 111–117 °C. Above that, loaded parts start to deform.

Does PC (polycarbonate) need to be dried?

Recommended: 80 °C, 8 h.

Do I need an enclosure for PC (polycarbonate)?

An enclosed chamber is strongly recommended, otherwise expect warping and layer cracks.
