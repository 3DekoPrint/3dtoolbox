Filament Materials Compared

Which filament fits your project? Properties, print settings and difficulty of all common materials – each with its own detail page.

Standard & PLA variants

PLA

very easy

Models, figures, prototypes, decor, everyday objects not exposed to heat

Nozzle (°C): 190–230 · Bed (°C): 25–65

View details →

PLA+ / PLA Pro / Tough PLA

very easy

Functional parts needing more impact toughness, holders, housings

Nozzle (°C): 190–230 · Bed (°C): 25–65

View details →

Silk PLA

very easy

Vases, decor, figures, gifts – anything that should shine

Nozzle (°C): 190–230 · Bed (°C): 25–65

View details →

Matte PLA

very easy

Display parts, housings, cosplay, photo props

Nozzle (°C): 190–230 · Bed (°C): 25–65

View details →

HT-PLA (heat-resistant PLA)

easy

Parts that should withstand ca. 100 °C after annealing

Nozzle (°C): 190–230 (per manufacturer) · Bed (°C): 55–65

View details →

LW-PLA (lightweight / foaming PLA)

medium

Model aircraft, light housings and decor

Nozzle (°C): 210–250 · Bed (°C): 25–60

View details →

Engineering

PETG

easy

Functional parts, brackets, containers, limited outdoor use, watertight parts

Nozzle (°C): 230–270 · Bed (°C): 60–80

View details →

ABS

medium

Mechanically stressed parts, housings, heat-exposed parts; smoothable with acetone

Nozzle (°C): 240–280 · Bed (°C): 90–100

View details →

ASA

medium

Outdoor: garden parts, brackets, car accessories, housings in sunlight

Nozzle (°C): 240–280 · Bed (°C): 75–100

View details →

PC (polycarbonate)

demanding

Heat and impact loaded parts: fan shrouds, levers, housings, hinges

Nozzle (°C): 260–290 · Bed (°C): 100–120

View details →

PA / Nylon (unfilled)

demanding

Gears, bearings, hinges, tool and machine parts

Nozzle (°C): approx. 260–285 · Bed (°C): approx. 100–110

View details →

PP (polypropylene)

experts only

Containers, lab parts, chemical-resistant and food-adjacent parts, living hinges

Nozzle (°C): 220–270 · Bed (°C): 80–105

View details →

Flexible

TPU 95A (flexible)

medium

Seals, bumpers, cases, hoses, wheel tyres, dampers

Nozzle (°C): 210–240 · Bed (°C): 25–60

View details →

Soft TPU (60A–90A)

demanding

Very soft seals, grips, shoe soles, stamps

Nozzle (°C): 230–245 · Bed (°C): 60–75

View details →

Fibre composites (carbon/glass)

PLA-CF (carbon fibre PLA)

easy

Stiff light parts, drone/RC parts, matte technical look

Nozzle (°C): 210–240 · Bed (°C): 45–65

View details →

PETG-CF (carbon fibre PETG)

easy

Stiff technical parts, brackets, jigs

Nozzle (°C): 240–270 · Bed (°C): 60–80

View details →

PA-CF (carbon fibre nylon: PA6-CF, PAHT-CF, PA11-CF)

demanding

Load-bearing technical parts: brackets, jigs, engine/machine parts, drones

Nozzle (°C): 260–300 · Bed (°C): 100–120

View details →

PA-GF (glass fibre nylon)

demanding

Stiff heat-resistant parts; glass fibre is less conductive than carbon

Nozzle (°C): 260–300 (per manufacturer) · Bed (°C): 80–120

View details →

PET-CF / PET-GF

demanding

Heat and dimensionally stable technical parts, less moisture-sensitive than nylon

Nozzle (°C): 260–300 · Bed (°C): 70–100

View details →

Effect (wood, metal, glow)

Wood filament (wood PLA)

easy

Decor, signs, jewellery, housings with a wood look; can be sanded and stained

Nozzle (°C): 190–210 (PolyWood); wood-powder filaments often up to 220 · Bed (°C): 25–60

View details →

Metal-filled filament

medium

Display/decor parts with a metal look, polishable

Nozzle (°C): 190–270 (depending on base) · Bed (°C): 60–100

View details →

Glow-in-the-dark

easy

Decor, toys, emergency/orientation markers

Nozzle (°C): as base material (PLA/PETG) · Bed (°C): as base material

View details →

Support (soluble)

PVA / BVOH (water-soluble)

demanding

Support material for complex overhangs (multi-material / dual nozzle)

Nozzle (°C): 195 (PVA+) / 215 (BVOH) · Bed (°C): 60

View details →

HIPS (limonene-soluble)

medium

Soluble support for PETG; light, sandable

Nozzle (°C): 225–255 · Bed (°C): 100–110

View details →

High-performance

High-performance: PEI (Ultem), PPS, PEEK, PEKK, PSU

experts only

Aerospace, industry, extreme heat and chemicals

Nozzle (°C): well above 300 (per manufacturer) · Bed (°C): per manufacturer (heated chamber)

View details →

Values are guidelines from manufacturer datasheets (Polymaker, Bambu Lab, Prusa). They may differ by manufacturer, colour and print settings.
