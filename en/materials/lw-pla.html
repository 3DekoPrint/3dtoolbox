What is LW-PLA (lightweight / foaming PLA) used for?

Model aircraft, light housings and decor

+ Pros

Foams during printing – much lighter parts

– Cons

Temperature and flow need calibration for target weight, rougher surface

Printing tips at a glance

  • Nozzle temperature: 210–250 °C – start in the middle of the range on a new spool and fine-tune with a temperature tower.
  • Bed temperature: 25–60 °C.
  • An enclosure is not needed, but avoid drafts.
  • Drying: 55 °C, 6–8 h. Wet filament shows up as crackling, stringing and rough surfaces.

Frequently asked questions

What nozzle temperature does LW-PLA (lightweight / foaming PLA) need?

Guideline for LW-PLA (lightweight / foaming PLA): 210–250 °C nozzle and 25–60 °C bed temperature. The exact values are printed on the manufacturer's spool.

How heat resistant is LW-PLA (lightweight / foaming PLA)?

Heat deflection temperature (HDT) is around approx. 54 °C. Above that, loaded parts start to deform.

Does LW-PLA (lightweight / foaming PLA) need to be dried?

Recommended: 55 °C, 6–8 h.

Do I need an enclosure for LW-PLA (lightweight / foaming PLA)?

An enclosure is not needed, but avoid drafts.
