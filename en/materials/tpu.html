What is TPU 95A (flexible) used for?

Seals, bumpers, cases, hoses, wheel tyres, dampers

+ Pros

Rubber-like, very high elongation, abrasion and chemical resistant, impact tough

– Cons

Print slowly (ca. 30–50 mm/s), direct drive recommended, stringing, moisture sensitive

Printing tips at a glance

  • Nozzle temperature: 210–240 °C – start in the middle of the range on a new spool and fine-tune with a temperature tower.
  • Bed temperature: 25–60 °C.
  • An enclosure is optional but helps against warping on large parts.
  • Drying: 70 °C, 8 h. Wet filament shows up as crackling, stringing and rough surfaces.
