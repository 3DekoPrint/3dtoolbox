What is HIPS (limonene-soluble) used for?

Soluble support for PETG; light, sandable

+ Pros

Dissolves in d-limonene, sticks well, smoothable with acetone

– Cons

Not suitable as support for ABS/ASA (they partly dissolve too), fumes, warping

Printing tips at a glance

  • Nozzle temperature: 225–255 °C – start in the middle of the range on a new spool and fine-tune with a temperature tower.
  • Bed temperature: 100–110 °C.
  • An enclosure is optional but helps against warping on large parts.
