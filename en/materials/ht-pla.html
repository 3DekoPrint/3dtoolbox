What is HT-PLA (heat-resistant PLA) used for?

Parts that should withstand ca. 100 °C after annealing

+ Pros

Much more heat-resistant than PLA after annealing (Polymaker: HDT ca. 107 °C)

– Cons

Annealing needed (part may shrink/warp slightly), extra effort

Printing tips at a glance

  • Nozzle temperature: 190–230 (per manufacturer) °C – start in the middle of the range on a new spool and fine-tune with a temperature tower.
  • Bed temperature: 55–65 °C.
  • An enclosure is not needed, but avoid drafts.
  • Drying: 55 °C, 6–8 h. Wet filament shows up as crackling, stringing and rough surfaces.

Frequently asked questions

What nozzle temperature does HT-PLA (heat-resistant PLA) need?

Guideline for HT-PLA (heat-resistant PLA): 190–230 (per manufacturer) °C nozzle and 55–65 °C bed temperature. The exact values are printed on the manufacturer's spool.

How heat resistant is HT-PLA (heat-resistant PLA)?

Heat deflection temperature (HDT) is around 61 (raw) / approx. 107 (annealed) °C. Above that, loaded parts start to deform.

Does HT-PLA (heat-resistant PLA) need to be dried?

Recommended: 55 °C, 6–8 h.

Do I need an enclosure for HT-PLA (heat-resistant PLA)?

An enclosure is not needed, but avoid drafts.
